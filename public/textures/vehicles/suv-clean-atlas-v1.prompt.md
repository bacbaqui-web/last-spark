# SUV clean atlas
Mode: GPT Image generation (new image). File: suv-clean-atlas-v1.png.

Production game diffuse UV atlas, flat orthographic technical surface artwork, six equal tiles in precisely two columns and three equal rows, square image. Generic compact 5-door SUV matching an early 2000s European SUV blueprint: tall broad cabin, gently curved roof, short rear overhang, long sloped hood, two side windows plus small rear quarter window, circular wheel wells, twin circular headlights on each side within one clear headlight housing, dark split center grille without badges. Every painted body surface must be saturated cyan teal (RGB roughly 30,180,180) so a game shader can identify and recolor it. Clean paint with subtle panel shading, NO RUST anywhere. All glass charcoal, tires black, wheel steel grey, headlights white, taillights red. Layout: TOP LEFT entire left side profile body front points LEFT, fill rectangular tile with body surface, no transparent silhouette gaps outside vehicle; TOP RIGHT entire top projection front points LEFT; MIDDLE LEFT front face grille headlights bumper; MIDDLE RIGHT rear face lamps tailgate bumper; BOTTOM LEFT centered circular steel wheel and black tire, dark surrounding background; BOTTOM RIGHT rubber tire tread closeup. Equal row height essential. No perspective, no text or badges, no borders or labels, no cast shadows. A UV surface texture image only, not a car render.

Paint tint is masked from cyan pixels. Rust is sampled separately from rusted-paint-v1.png with a dark corrosion mask, independent of the selected paint color. This is a blueprint-inspired game prototype, not a dimensionally exact CAD reconstruction.
